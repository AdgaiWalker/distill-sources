i tend to use my swarm for refactoring, for example i made a skill that goes through all the commits to the grok bot codebase and checks if we let any slop or perf regressions in. if we do i add new lint rules to guard against them

for other work i tend to try and think from the user’s pov, what impacts them the most? usually it’s bug fixes, sometimes it’s adding small quality of life things. i try to think about the impact of the work relative to the mental bandwidth id need to own it
