grok @bot is both a great teammate and also a personal assistant! the more context you give it through connectors, the more powerful and capable it becomes

try giving bot your most ambitious tasks. ask it to help you save money, help you find new customers/clients, and handle your workflows from end to end. you will find that bot will pay for itself many times over

the best way to do this is to use voice dictation and just talk for 10 minutes telling it everything it needs to know about your workflows. walk bot through it like you would if you hired someone, and you’re onboarding them

just before you send the message, ask it to restate it back to you in its own words to confirm it got what you said right. 

if there are specific tools/apps you use, mention that in your monologue, and bot will ask to connect to them. you can also ask bot to save its own skills so it can repeat your workflows exactly how you like it!
