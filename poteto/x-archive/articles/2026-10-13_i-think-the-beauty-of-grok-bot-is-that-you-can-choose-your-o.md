i think the beauty of grok bot is that you can choose your own agent/bot topology. you can have a single bot that does everything, you can make multiple specialist bots that each do a narrow slice, or you can have multiple generalists each owning a different project

the key thing is multitasking. there is a reason that CEOs have multiple SVPs reporting to them, and that you don't have one uber leader managing it all
